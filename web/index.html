itemize

Check an itemized medical bill against public federal data — duplicate charges, unspecified codes, and drug prices compared to what Medicare allows.

Your bill never leaves this device. There is no upload, no account and no server. Everything below runs in your browser, and you can disconnect from the internet before pasting if you prefer. The page works offline once loaded.

1. Your situation

Who is exposed to the charge changes which findings matter — and it decides whether you have rights that outrank any coding question. Every answer is optional and nothing is assumed if you skip it. These answers stay on this device.

This one question decides whether your state's protections reach you at all — state insurance law generally does not apply to self-funded employer plans. Your HR or benefits booklet will say which you have.

2. Paste your itemized bill

A spreadsheet export (CSV) works best. Text copied out of a PDF also works. If your statement shows only department totals, that is not an itemized bill — ask the billing office for one showing a code, quantity and charge on every line.

3. Your Explanation of Benefits (optional, but the strongest check)

If you are insured, add the EOB your plan sent. If the provider is billing you more than the EOB says you owe, that is the clearest error there is — and it is the one your insurer will not catch for you.

What this is, and what it is not.

itemize is an open-source tool. It gives you information, not advice — not legal, medical or financial advice — and it cannot tell you what you owe.

  • A Medicare benchmark is not a price cap. Hospitals are not required to bill Medicare rates, and commercial rates are legitimately higher. A large multiple is grounds to ask questions and to request financial assistance. It is not proof of an error.
  • No findings does not mean the bill is correct. It means these particular checks did not fire.
  • Lines coded with CPT codes are not benchmarked here. Those codes are copyright the American Medical Association and this tool does not redistribute them. See NOTICE and the itemize ncci command.
