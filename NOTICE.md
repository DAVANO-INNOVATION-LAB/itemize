# Third-party rights and what this repository does not contain

`itemize` is deliberately built around a licensing boundary. Read this before
adding data to the repository.

## What is in this repository

`web/data/*.json` is generated by `tools/build_data.py` and contains **only**:

* **HCPCS Level II codes and descriptions** — alpha-numeric codes with prefixes
  A, B, C, E, G, H, J, K, L, M, P, Q, R, S, T, U, V. These are authored and
  jointly maintained by CMS and are United States government work product.
* **Medicare Part B ASP payment limits** — dollar amounts computed and published
  by CMS. These are facts about federal payment policy.

`manifest.json` records the source URL, file name, SHA-256 and retrieval date of
every file the data was derived from, so any claim the tool makes can be traced
back to a specific federal file.

## What is deliberately excluded

### CPT — American Medical Association

Level I HCPCS codes (five-digit numeric), CPT Category II (`nnnnF`) and Category
III (`nnnnT`) codes and **all** their descriptions are copyright the American
Medical Association. CMS states the rule in the HCPCS record layout distributed
with the data:

> CPT-4 codes including both long and short descriptions shall be used in
> accordance with the CMS/AMA agreement. Any other use violates the AMA
> copyright.

`tools/build_data.py` filters these out. They are never written to disk.

### CDT — American Dental Association

Level II `Dnnnn` dental codes and descriptors are copyright the American Dental
Association, per the same CMS record layout. Also filtered out.

### NCCI Procedure-to-Procedure edit files

CMS distributes these behind a click-through AMA licence at
`/license/ama?file=...` because they contain CPT codes. They are **not** shipped
here and are **not** fetched silently.

The `itemize ncci` command shows you where CMS's licence lives, requires you to
type `accept`, records that acceptance under `~/.cache/itemize/`, and only then
downloads the files — to your machine, under your licence, not the project's.
The underlying file URLs are trivially reachable without the click-through. We
do not use them that way, and neither should a fork.

## Consequences for redistribution

* You **may** host `web/` publicly, including on GitHub Pages. Everything in
  `web/data/` is US government work product.
* You **may not** add CPT or CDT codes or descriptions to this repository, or to
  a fork you publish, without a licence from the AMA or ADA respectively.
* A **hosted service** that performs CPT-based analysis for users needs its own
  AMA distribution licence. That is exactly the cost commercial bill-negotiation
  services have already paid, and it is why the CPT tier here is local-only.

If you believe any file in `web/data/` contains third-party-copyrighted content,
open an issue and it will be removed while the claim is checked.

## Government works

Publications of the United States Government are not subject to copyright
protection in the United States (17 U.S.C. § 105). CMS's own HCPCS Level II
codes and published payment amounts fall in that category. This does not extend
to the AMA and ADA material CMS redistributes under licence, which is why the
filter in `tools/build_data.py` exists.
